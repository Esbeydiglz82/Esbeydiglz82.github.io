About My Collection

I am selling my prized romance, fantasy, and thriller book collection to help pay my tuition bill. I have collected these books throughout my life, and they include some of my favorite stories and authors.

The Empyrean Series

The Empyrean Series by Rebecca Yarros is one of my favorite collections. It includes the Fourth Wing books as well as the newer Empyrean-related book, Threshing Day. The series is about a brutal military academy where young cadets train to bond with dragons and fight in a deadly war.

The Matched Series

The Matched Series is another series in my collection. It is about a strictly controlled future society where government officials choose citizens' jobs, life partners, and lifespans.

Thriller Books

I also collect books by Lisa Jewell. Her books are about ordinary people with hidden secrets, missing persons, and domestic suspense. Her books are some of the darker and more suspenseful books in my collection.

I hate the thought of parting with my books, but I am selling them to help with college expenses. I hope the books go to someone who will love them as much as I have.
