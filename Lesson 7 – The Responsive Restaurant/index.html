From Our Kitchen

Gather around the table and enjoy our favorite Mexican dishes.

Street Tacos

Three warm corn tortillas filled with grilled chicken and topped with onions and cilantro. Served with fresh lime and homemade salsa.

Enchiladas Rojas

Corn tortillas filled with shredded chicken, covered in red chile sauce and melted cheese. Served with rice and beans.

Cinnamon Churros

Warm, crispy churros rolled in cinnamon sugar and served with chocolate dipping sauce. A sweet & yummy finish to your meal! (Ask for vinilla icecream on the side to elevate this even more!)
