About Our Store

Store Hours

Monday–Saturday: 10 AM–6 PM

Golden Groove Records is an independent record store in the small town of New Cumberland, Pennsylvania. We bring music fans together through a shared love of vinyl records and music!

Our store offers different types of music, including rock, pop, jazz, and folk. We welcome new collectors and people who have enjoyed records for years to come check out our shop!

Visit us to browse our featured albums, discover new artists, and find your next favorite record.

Our Mission

Helping people discover great music, one record at a time.
