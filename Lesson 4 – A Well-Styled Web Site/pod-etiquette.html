Talk to your pod before you play

A pod is your group of players. Before a casual game, make sure everyone is comfortable with proxies.

Questions to ask your group

  • Is everyone okay with using proxies?
  • Can everyone read and identify the proxy cards?
  • Are our decks at a similar power level?
  • Does our group want any limits on proxies?

What about tournaments?

Player-made proxies are generally NOT ALLOWED in sanctioned Magic tournaments. Judges may issue replacements in limited situations, but a homemade playtest proxy is not the same thing. Check an event's rules before bringing your deck.

You could say: “I'm trying a few paper proxies while I learn this deck. Is everyone okay with that?”
