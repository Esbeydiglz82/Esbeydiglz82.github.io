Learn Magic: The Gathering with proxies

Interested in Magic but unsure which cards you want to buy? This guide explains how beginners can use proxies to try cards while learning with friends.

What is a proxy?

A proxy is a stand-in for a Magic card, such as a clearly labeled piece of paper placed in a card sleeve. It lets you try a card in a casual game without owning it.

Why try proxies?

Save money

Magic decks can be expensive. Proxies let you try a deck before deciding whether to buy the real cards.

Experiment with cards

When you are new, it takes time to discover your favorite playstyle. Testing cards first helps you avoid buying ones that do not fit your deck.

Play at your group's level

In a casual pod that agrees to proxies, new players can try cards that help their decks keep up with more experienced players.

Learn by playing

Trying different cards and interactions in a game can help you understand them better than reading about them alone.

Helpful tool: ManaBox

ManaBox can help you build a deck, explore cards, and check card prices. After trying a deck with your pod, you can use it to find marketplace links for real cards you decide to buy.
