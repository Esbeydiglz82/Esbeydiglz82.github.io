Getting Started With Proxies

A proxy is a stand-in for a (MTG) Magic The Gathering card. You can make one to try out a card or deck before buying the original. Many people print the card on regular paper and place it in a sleeve with a card they are not using to make it sturdier, but that is not necessary.

Make Your Proxy Easy to Understand

Other players should be able to identify the card & understand what it does. Include its name and the rules text from the original card. The art can be changed to fit the theme of your deck.
