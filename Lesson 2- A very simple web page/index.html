About my collection

I am selling my prized book romance/fantasy collection to help pay my tuition bill. I have collected these books throughout my life and they include some of my favorite stories!

The books

My collection includes many popular romance and fantasy books such as Onyx Storm, Ugly Love, The Love Hypothesis & many more! Most of the books are in good/used condition and have only been read a few times.

Why I am selling

I hate the thought of parting with all my books, but I am selling them to help with college expenses. I hope all the books go to someone who will enjoy them as much as I have enjoyed reading them.
